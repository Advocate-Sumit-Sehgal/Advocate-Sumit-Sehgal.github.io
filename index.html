About

I provide clear, business-focused legal support to founders, teams, and individuals. My work is simple: protect you early, defend you when required.

I focus on outcomes, not drama — strong agreements, reduced exposure, faster resolution.

Practice Areas
  • Contracts & Compliance: Drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial agreements so obligations are clear and enforceable.
  • Employment & HR Advisory: Offer letters, separations, internal policies, non-disclosure and non-compete language.
  • Business Disputes: Legal notices, settlement strategy, representation in commercial and civil matters.
  • Regulatory Guidance: Confidentiality, data handling, and risk posture for growing teams and early-stage companies.
How I Work
  • Prevent first: Strong paperwork so you don't bleed later.
  • Business-friendly: Contracts that protect you without killing the deal.
  • Direct answers: Clear next steps, not 5 pages of jargon.
  • Escalate when needed: If someone stops respecting terms, I make sure they start respecting them.
