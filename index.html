About

Focused on outcomes, not drama

Adv. Sumit Sehgal
Civil, criminal, corporate and family litigation. Strategic legal counsel.
I advise businesses, founders, and individuals on how to protect themselves before problems escalate — and I represent clients aggressively when they do.

My approach is direct: strong paperwork at the start, pressure where required, and disciplined execution in court. No noise, no 20-page lectures. Clear next steps.

I regularly handle: complex civil/commercial disputes, cheque bounce / 138 NI Act matters, high-stakes terminations and labour disputes, FIR defence and criminal allegations (including white collar), family/matrimonial litigation, and domestic & international arbitration.
Counsel in Practice
Representation. Strategy. Courtroom Work.
Active litigation practice across civil, commercial, criminal, labour, family, corporate and technology matters. In court, in conference, and in advisory mode.
Courtroom Representation
Formal appearance and argument in complex matters. Cross-examination, recovery, injunctions.
Trial and Appellate Lawyer
Prepared, documented, and on record — filings, appeals, written submissions. Not just advisory.
Strategy & Advisory
High-stakes disputes are won in preparation, negotiation leverage, and risk control — not improvisation.
On-Ground Litigation
Real clients, real cases, real consequences. FIR defence, cheque bounce, labour disputes, enforcement.
